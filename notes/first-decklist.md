# First decklist (built from borg3)

`borg3.txt` was the strongest and most recent of the three builds, so the first
real list is borg3 with the minimum change to make it legal:

- **Out: Biotransference.** borg3 had 101 cards. Biotransference was the weakest
  card: almost every creature here is already an artifact, and it costs life on
  every artifact spell.
- **Renamed:** Central Plexus Core → The Immortal Sun (same card, Scryfall name).

All 100 cards checked on Scryfall: legal in Commander and inside Esper color identity.

## Shape of the deck
- 23 artifact creatures (including the Queen), plus 7 Spacecraft/vehicles that become creatures
- 32 lands plus 4 modal cards that can be lands (Sea Gate Restoration, Sink into Stupor,
  Fell the Profane, Witch Enchanter)
- ~11 mana rocks and mana creatures
- Interaction: Anguished Unmaking, Void Rend, Generous Gift, Fell the Profane, Dispatch,
  Cyclonic Rift, Sink into Stupor, Witch Enchanter, Mana Drain, Force of Negation, Swan Song
- One-sided wipes: Battle of Wolf 359, Crystalline Entity
- Protection: Teferi's Protection, Clever Concealment, Cryptothrall, Padeem, Bronze Guardian

## Ideas from borg1/borg2 that didn't make it (worth testing)
- Cyberdrive Awakener: team flying as a finisher
- Hugh, First of Many and Iron Spider, Stark Upgrade
- Research Thief / Sharding Sphinx for more combat-damage payoffs
- Cheap counters: Dovin's Veto, An Offer You Can't Refuse, Disallow
- Fellwar Stone, Toxic Deluge, Emergency Regeneration, Collective Override
