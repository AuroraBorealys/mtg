# Card-by-card review (2026-11-23)

Every card was judged on one question: **what job does it do in this deck, and is it
the best card for that job?** EDHREC popularity, synergy scores and "it's a staple"
did not count as reasons. Cards from the earlier builds (borg3 > borg1 > borg2) got
weight because they've been played; a new card had to beat them on function.

Tags: **(1/2/3)** = was in borg1/borg2/borg3. **NEW** = not in any earlier build.

## Cut (15)
| Card | Why it's out |
|------|--------------|
| Walking Ballista (NEW) | Pings only scale with +1/+1 counters, and the anthems don't add counters; no combo partner here. A 2-mana 1/1 otherwise. |
| Skullclamp (NEW) | Fights the deck: once Tempered Steel, Master of Etherium or the Queen is out, tokens are 2/2–3/3 and Clamp no longer kills them. Only works before the anthems land. |
| Retrofitter Foundry (NEW) | Was here to feed Skullclamp. Without Clamp it's a mana-hungry 1/1 factory. |
| Chrome Mox (NEW) | Costs a card from hand to ramp one mana. An aggro deck that empties its hand can't afford that. |
| Vampiric Tutor, Enlightened Tutor (NEW) | No combo to find. Putting a card on top costs a card and a draw. Demonic stays as a clean "find Tempered Steel / Krang / Teferi's." |
| Antagonist Incorporator (NEW) | Pay {1} per hit for a 1/1 — same job Locutus of Borg does for free. |
| Jurati, Cordial Queen (NEW) | Needs to connect and needs a good token to copy. Sai makes a token every artifact spell with no conditions. |
| Hugh, First of Many (1) | Replicate costs {2}{U} per copy. Iron Man copies once a turn for free. |
| Phyrexian Metamorph (NEW) | Copying One, Krang, Locutus or Iron Man hits the legend rule; Iron Man and Ultron already cover copying. |
| Hive Mind Coprocessor (NEW) | Draw on hit for life — Co-Option Adjutant already does it for free. |
| Class-4 Tactical Cube (2, 3) | Its +1/+1 anthem only turns on after stationing 7 power, which means tapping attackers. |
| Deliver from Chaos (NEW) | 5 mana removal. Generous Gift does the job for 3 and hits any permanent. |
| Borg Incursion (NEW) | Protection from a color doesn't stop "destroy all" wipes. Emergency Regeneration and Clever Concealment do. |
| Crystalline Entity (1, 3) | 8-mana wipe that only matters when the cast trigger resolves. Battle of Wolf 359 does that job for 5, and the top-end slot is better spent on Krang. |

## Kept (84 of the 99, including 31 lands) — and the reason
**Cheap army and mana:** Esper Sentinel (1/2/3; 1-drop that taxes or draws), Ornithopter
of Paradise (NEW; ramp and fixing that's also an evasive artifact creature for the anthems),
Command Subadjunct (1/2/3; team vigilance lets the army attack and then still tap for
Ambulatory Reactor mana or block), Locutus, the One Who Speaks (NEW; 2-mana +1/+1 lord, or
removal from hand when you need it), Etherium Sculptor and Foundry Inspector (1/2/3;
cost reducers), Extraction Integrant (1/2/3; team menace), Steel Overseer (NEW; permanent
counters on the whole team every turn), Ambulatory Reactor (1/2/3; every artifact
creature becomes mana for artifact spells — the engine that makes Krang and Squadron castable).

**Lords and team abilities:** Master of Etherium, Skorpekh Lord, Co-Option Adjutant,
Primary Unimatrix Adjunct, Locutus of Borg, One, Borg Evolved (all 1/2/3).

**Big threats:** Bronze Guardian (1/2/3; I'd underrated it — double strike with power equal
to your artifact count, and ward 2 on every artifact), Karn, Legacy Reforged (1/2/3; huge
artifact-only mana every upkeep).

**Other creatures:** Losheel (1/2/3; card a turn and attack with no risk), Urza, Lord High
Artificer (2/3; artifacts become blue mana, plus a big Construct), Urza, Chief Artificer
(1/2/3; menace plus a growing Construct every end step).

**Mana rocks:** Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, three Talismans (1/2/3); Mana Vault (NEW; burst
mana toward the 7–9 drops); Mox Opal (NEW; metalcraft is on by turn 2 here).

**Engines:** Tempered Steel (NEW; +2/+2 for 3 mana, the best anthem that isn't the Queen),
Conquest Mission (NEW; a 1/1 for every nontoken artifact — with anthems each one is a real
attacker), Rhystic Study, Sea Gate Restoration (1/2/3), Thoughtcast (1), The One Ring (NEW; protection for a turn and a draw engine for
a deck that dumps its hand).

**Tutor:** Demonic Tutor (NEW; the one tutor that doesn't cost a card).

**Interaction:** Anguished Unmaking, Sink into Stupor (1/2/3), Dispatch, Fell the Profane,
Swan Song, Force of Negation, Mana Drain, Cyclonic Rift (from 1–3), An Offer You Can't Refuse
(2), Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Counterspell (NEW; one-mana-cheaper answers than
what they replaced).

**Wipe:** Battle of Wolf 359 (1/2/3; one-sided).

**Protection:** Teferi's Protection (1/2/3), Clever Concealment (3), Emergency Regeneration (1).

**Lands (31):** kept as built — untapped fixing first (original duals, shocks, fetches,
check lands, Verges, Raffine's Tower), three artifact lands for Mox Opal and affinity, and
Urza's Saga, Academy Ruins, Spire of Industry and Otawara as utility. (Tapped lands changed in the second pass below.)

## Second pass after Gemini's review (2026-11-23)
See `notes/gemini-review.md` for Gemini's arguments. Claude first applied most of them, judging
cards on raw efficiency. The user then set out the deck's four jobs (now in `AGENTS.md`):
spread abilities like slivers, make artifact creature tokens, buff them, and get advantage
from artifacts. Judged by those jobs, several of Gemini's cuts were wrong, so the final
result is smaller.

**Changed:**
| Out | In | Why |
|-----|----|-----|
| Unwinding Clock (1/2/3) | Galadriel's Dismissal (NEW) | Reactor mana only pays for artifacts, and the deck has almost nothing to cast on opponents' turns. Kicked Dismissal phases out your creatures in response to a wipe (not rocks or lands), covering what Cryptothrall's hexproof can't. |
| Ultron, Artificial Malevolence (1/2/3) | Phyrexian Metamorph (NEW) | Ultron had the same legend-rule problem I cut Metamorph for, and its copied rocks are summoning sick. Metamorph is cheaper and copies anything, including tokens makers and lords. |
| Thirst for Knowledge (1) | Thought Monitor (NEW) | Job 4: gets cheaper with every artifact, draws two, leaves a 2/2 flying artifact creature. |
| The Cyber-Controller (1/2) | Tezzeret, Master of the Bridge (1) | User: the +1/+1 is nice, the token side is mediocre. Tezzeret is job 4: creature spells get affinity for artifacts (Krang gets much cheaper), and it drains each opponent for your artifact count. |

**Kept after all (Gemini wanted them out):**
- **Cyberman Patrol** (job 1): afflict 3 triggers on every blocked attacker, so with a wide
  board each opponent takes the combat damage or loses 3 per creature they block.
- **Cryptothrall** (job 1): team hexproof.
- **Krang, Utrom Warlord** (job 1): team flying, trample, indestructible, haste. Still
  expensive; Tezzeret makes it cheaper.
- **Thopter Spy Network** (jobs 2 and 4): thopter tokens and card draw.
- **The Immortal Sun** (jobs 3 and 4): +1/+1, an extra card each turn, spells cost 1 less.

**Tried and removed:** Baleful Strix (efficient, but does none of the four jobs), Cyberdrive
Awakener (overlaps Krang), Bident of Thassa (overlaps Thopter Spy Network), Kappa Cannoneer
(a strong lone threat, but doesn't spread abilities or make tokens), Tezzeret the Seeker.

**Lands (user's rule: no tapped lands unless there's a real reason, like being an artifact):**
| Out | In | Why |
|-----|----|-----|
| Deserted Beach | Goldmire Bridge (2, 3) | Bridges enter tapped but are artifacts (Mox Opal, Thought Monitor, Tezzeret, affinity) and indestructible, so they survive artifact wipes. |
| Shipwreck Marsh | Mistvault Bridge (2, 3) | Same. |
| Shattered Sanctum | Razortide Bridge (2, 3) | Same. |
| Raffine's Tower (2) | Vault of Champions (NEW) | Raffine's Tower always enters tapped. Vault of Champions enters untapped with two or more opponents. |

Not decided yet: Battle of Wolf 359 vs a wipe that works from behind, Salvation Engine vs
Simulacrum Synthesizer, Urza's Saga targets, and graveyard hate.

**Not yet verified on Scryfall:** Galadriel's Dismissal, Phyrexian Metamorph, Thought Monitor,
Tezzeret, Master of the Bridge and Vault of Champions. Check text, legality and color identity before the next game.
