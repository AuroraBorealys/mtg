# Deck plan: The Borg Queen, high-power artifact creature tribal

Every slot was judged on the job it does in this deck. Cards from the earlier, played
builds (borg3 > borg1 > borg2) got weight because they've been tested; EDHREC numbers and
"it's a staple" weren't accepted as reasons.

## Core principle: the deck must win without the Queen
The Borg Queen costs 7 and will often be removed or taxed at a high-power table.
The 99 is built as a strong artifact-creature aggro deck on its own; the Queen is a
bonus that makes a winning board win faster, not a piece the plan depends on.
- No cards that only work while the commander is out (e.g. Fierce Guardianship,
  Deadly Rollick), and no commander-protection equipment (Lightning Greaves,
  Swiftfoot Boots).
- No cards picked just because they're Borg-flavored; each one must be good on its own.
- Every anthem, draw engine and finisher works with or without her.

## How the deck wins
1. Turns 1–3: fast mana and cheap artifact creatures, ideally a cost reducer.
2. Turns 3–5: lords (Tempered Steel, Master of Etherium, Locutus the One Who Speaks,
   Skorpekh Lord, Salvation Engine, Wanderglyph) turn the small bodies into a real army;
   Rhystic Study, Bident of Thassa, The One Ring and combat-damage triggers refill the hand.
3. Finish with One, Borg Evolved (flying, double strike, lifelink, menace for the team),
   Primary Unimatrix Adjunct (double strike), Cyberdrive Awakener (team flying, rocks become
   attackers) or Cybermen Squadron (every attacker hits all three opponents). Tezzeret,
   Master of the Bridge drains each opponent for your artifact count without attacking.
4. If the Queen is cast, she's another +2/+2 anthem that steals a creature every attack.

## Big-mana engine
Karn, Ambulatory Reactor and Urza, Lord High Artificer turn the army itself into mana for
artifact spells, and Tezzeret, Master of the Bridge, Thought Monitor and Kappa Cannoneer get
cheaper with every artifact out. Cybermen Squadron is still the deck's most expensive card.

Every card's reason for being in or out is in `notes/card-review.md`.
