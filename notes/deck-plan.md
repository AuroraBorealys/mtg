# Deck plan: The Borg Queen, high-power artifact creature tribal

Built from the role each slot needs, not from the earlier lists. The full card pool
was considered (EDHREC's most popular and highest-synergy cards for The Borg Queen,
plus high-power staples). A card from borg1–3 stayed only if it was still the best
option for its slot; 62 of borg3's cards survived, 38 slots are new.

## Core principle: the deck must win without the Queen
The Borg Queen costs 7 and will often be removed or taxed at a high-power table.
The 99 is built as a strong artifact-creature aggro deck on its own; the Queen is a
bonus that makes a winning board win faster, not a piece the plan depends on.
- No cards that only work while the commander is out (e.g. Fierce Guardianship,
  Deadly Rollick), and no commander-protection equipment (Lightning Greaves,
  Swiftfoot Boots).
- No cards picked just because they're Borg-flavored; each one must be good on its own.
- Every anthem, draw engine and finisher works with or without her.

## How the deck wins
1. Turns 1–3: fast mana and cheap artifact creatures, ideally a cost reducer.
2. Turns 3–5: lords (Tempered Steel, Master of Etherium, Locutus the One Who Speaks,
   Skorpekh Lord) turn the small bodies into a real army; Skullclamp + tokens,
   Rhystic Study, The One Ring and combat-damage triggers refill the hand.
3. Finish with One, Borg Evolved (flying, double strike, lifelink, menace for the team)
   or Primary Unimatrix Adjunct (double strike), with protection held up for the wipe.
4. If the Queen is cast, she's another +2/+2 anthem that steals a creature every attack.

## New cards and why
**Fast mana:** Mana Vault, Mox Opal (metalcraft is easy here), Chrome Mox. Urza's Saga
fetches Sol Ring, Mana Vault, either Mox or Skullclamp and makes big Constructs.
**Army:** Ornithopter of Paradise (ramp that counts as an artifact creature), Steel Overseer,
Walking Ballista, Antagonist Incorporator (tokens on hit), Locutus, the One Who Speaks
(2-mana +1/+1 lord, or removal), Jurati, Cordial Queen (copies tokens on hit),
Hugh, First of Many (replicate doubles every artifact spell), Phyrexian Metamorph (copy
One or a lord), Hive Mind Coprocessor (every artifact creature draws on hit).
**Engines:** Tempered Steel (a second Queen anthem for 3), Skullclamp, Conquest Mission
(token per artifact), Retrofitter Foundry (1-mana token factory, Urza's Saga can fetch it),
The One Ring, Thoughtcast.
**Tutors:** Demonic, Vampiric, Enlightened.
**Interaction:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Deliver from Chaos (exile + tokens),
An Offer You Can't Refuse, Counterspell.
**Protection:** Borg Incursion, Emergency Regeneration.
**Lands:** Tundra, Underground Sea, Scrubland (no budget limit), Raffine's Tower,
check lands, Morphic Pool, Otawara, Urza's Saga.

## Cut from borg3 and why
- **Commander-dependent:** cards that only matter with the Queen out.
- **Too slow or clunky for high power:** Krang (9 mana), Cybermen Squadron, Borg Cube,
  Salvation Engine, Forsaken Monument (mostly colored creatures here), Illustrious
  Wanderglyph, Biotransference.
- **Spacecraft package** (Borg Scout Sphere, Type-03, The Seriema, Uthros Research Craft,
  Uthros Godcore, Adagia, Susur Secundi): stationing taps creatures that should be attacking.
- **Weaker card draw:** Fateful Discovery, One with the Machine, Traverse Eternity.
- **Replaced by better versions:** Thought Vessel, The Mind Stone, The Soul Stone (by Moxen and
  Mana Vault); Void Rend, Generous Gift, Witch Enchanter (by cheaper removal); Padeem
  (by Cryptothrall + protection spells); Smothering Tithe, Sai, Ultron, Iron Man,
  Simulacrum Synthesizer, Unwinding Clock, Cyberman Patrol.
- **Painlands and bridges** swapped for duals, check lands and Raffine's Tower.
